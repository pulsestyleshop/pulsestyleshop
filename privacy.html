Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

MortgagePro is designed with privacy at its core. We do not require accounts, and all mortgage calculations run entirely in your web browser. The numbers you enter are never transmitted to our servers.

Information We Collect

Calculator Inputs

All values you enter into the calculator (home price, down payment, interest rate, etc.) are processed locally in your browser. They are never sent to us or stored anywhere.

Contact Form

If you use our contact form, you voluntarily provide your name, email, and message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site usage. No personally identifiable financial data is ever tracked.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. Any cookies used are strictly for essential site functionality (such as remembering UI preferences).

Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Rights

  • You may request deletion of any contact information you've submitted
  • You may contact us at any time with privacy-related questions

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact us.
