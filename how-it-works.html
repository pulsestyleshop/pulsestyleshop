The Mortgage Formula

Monthly principal & interest is calculated using the standard amortization formula:

M = P × [ r(1 + r)ⁿ ] ÷ [ (1 + r)ⁿ − 1 ]
  • M = monthly payment (principal & interest)
  • P = loan principal (home price − down payment)
  • r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = total number of payments (years × 12)

What Each Input Means

Home Price

The total purchase price of the property.

Down Payment

The amount you pay upfront. A larger down payment reduces your loan amount and monthly payment.

Loan Term

The number of years over which you repay the loan. Longer terms mean lower monthly payments but more total interest.

Interest Rate

The annual interest rate on your loan, as quoted by your lender.

Property Tax, Insurance & HOA

These recurring costs are added to your monthly payment, often collected in an escrow account.
