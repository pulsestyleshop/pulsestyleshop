Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about mortgages and using our calculator.

How accurate is this mortgage calculator?
Our calculator uses the standard amortization formula used by lenders. Results are accurate estimates for principal and interest, plus any taxes, insurance, and HOA you enter. Your actual payment may differ due to lender fees, PMI, and closing costs.
What is a down payment?
A down payment is the portion of the home price you pay upfront. The rest is financed through a mortgage. A larger down payment reduces your loan amount and monthly payment, and may help you avoid PMI.
What is PMI and does this calculator include it?
PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is typically required when your down payment is less than 20%. Our basic calculator does not include PMI automatically — you can add it manually via the Advanced section if needed.
Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year loan?
A 15-year loan has higher monthly payments but much less total interest. A 30-year loan offers lower monthly payments but more interest over time. Choose based on your budget and long-term plans.
Does the calculator include property taxes and insurance?
Yes — expand the "Advanced" section to enter annual property tax, annual home insurance, and monthly HOA fees. These will be included in your monthly estimate and chart.
Is my data stored or shared?
No. All calculations happen in your browser. We do not store, transmit, or share any of the numbers you enter. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I see the amortization schedule?
Yes. On the calculator page, click "Show Amortization Schedule" to view a year-by-year breakdown of principal, interest, and remaining balance.
